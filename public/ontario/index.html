How local search works across the GTA

Google decides who appears in the map results using three things: how relevant your business is to the search, how close you are to the searcher, and how prominent you are (reviews, links and mentions across the web). Distance is the one you cannot change, which is why a business in Etobicoke rarely tops the map for Scarborough. What you can change is relevance and prominence, and that is where the work goes.

For most Ontario businesses that means a complete Google Business Profile with the right primary category, consistent name, address and phone number on every directory, steady reviews from real customers, and a website with a dedicated page for each service and each area you genuinely serve. On top of that, AI search tools such as Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT increasingly answer local questions directly, and they draw on the same clear, well-structured information.

What a first month usually looks like

  1. Audit: a technical and on-page check of every page, plus a look at your Business Profile and top local competitors.
  2. Fixes: speed, structured data, titles and headings, and anything blocking Google from understanding the site.
  3. Local foundation: Business Profile categories, services and photos, citation clean-up and a simple review request process.
  4. Tracking: calls, forms and chats connected to the searches that produced them, so you can judge results on enquiries rather than traffic.
