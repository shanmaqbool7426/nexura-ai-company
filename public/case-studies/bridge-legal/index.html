Case Study: Bridge Legal Services, Etobicoke

Bridge Legal Services is a licensed paralegal firm (Law Society of Ontario) that defends drivers against traffic tickets and Highway Traffic Act charges, with offices in Etobicoke, Mississauga and Brampton. Their site is bridgelegalservices.ca.

The problem

The site already had strong content, but on a phone the main headline and introduction stayed invisible until the page's JavaScript finished loading. Google's mobile test measured 8.1 seconds before the main content appeared. The structured data also marked up the firm's own Google reviews as a site rating, which Google does not allow and never shows as stars.

What we changed

  • Made the headline and introduction visible in the first HTML the server sends, instead of fading them in with JavaScript.
  • Rewrote the main heading to match what people search for: "Traffic Ticket Paralegal Toronto & GTA".
  • Removed the self-serving review markup and linked the schema to the firm's real Google Business Profile.
  • Replaced a 297 KB logo with a 6 KB version, and fixed form labels, heading order and text contrast.
  • Checked all 69 pages against 47 on-page SEO checks and fixed every issue found.

What comes next

Faster pages and clean structured data are the foundation. Rankings and enquiries build over the following months through the Google Business Profile, reviews and local content, and we will add those results here once they are measured.
